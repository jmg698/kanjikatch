## Capturing

### Does handwriting work?

Yes. KanjiKatch reads handwritten notes as well as printed text, screenshots, and manga panels. Clear, even lighting and a photo taken straight above the page give the best results. See [Capturing Japanese from photos and text](/help/capturing-japanese).

### KanjiKatch got a reading or meaning wrong. What can I do?

Before you save, you can edit any reading or meaning on the **Review what we found** screen. If you spot a mistake later on a vocabulary card, set it aside during a review with **The info looks wrong**, and KanjiKatch will try to fix the definition. See [Setting aside, fixing, or deleting a card](/help/set-aside-fix-delete-cards).

### I captured a page twice. Will I get duplicate cards?

No. If a word is already in your library, KanjiKatch merges it with your existing card instead of creating a new one.

### Does a failed capture use up an extraction?

No. An extraction only counts when KanjiKatch successfully reads your capture.

## Reviewing

### How many reviews should I do each day?

As many as are due. KanjiKatch only shows you cards when they're ready, so clearing your due cards each day is the goal. On a busy day, even a 5-card session keeps your streak going.

### I missed a few days. What happens?

Nothing is lost. Cards that came due while you were away wait for you, and the most overdue ones come first. Your streak starts again at 1 on your next review. If the pile looks big, select **Customize session** on your dashboard and review in smaller batches.

### Why did a card come back in the same session?

When you choose **Again** or **Hard**, the card returns a few cards later so you can lock it in. Only your first answer counts toward the card's schedule.

### I pressed the wrong grade button. Can I undo it?

Yes. Select **Redo last card**, or press **U**, right away. You can undo your most recent answer.

### Why does my streak look different from my local date?

Streak days are counted in UTC (Coordinated Universal Time). Depending on your time zone, a new "day" may start in the afternoon or evening. See [Tracking your streak and progress](/help/streaks-and-progress).

## Library

### How do I delete a card?

Set the card aside during a review (press **S**), then go to **Library** › **Set aside** and select **Delete**. See [Setting aside, fixing, or deleting a card](/help/set-aside-fix-delete-cards).

### Can I add a word without capturing a page?

Yes. Tap any new word in a practice sentence and select **Add to My Vocabulary**. See [Reading practice: See It In The Wild](/help/see-it-in-the-wild).

## Plans and account

### Is KanjiKatch free?

Yes. Reviews are unlimited on the free plan, and you get 10 welcome extractions plus 5 more each month. Pro removes the limit on captures. See [Free vs Pro: plans, limits, and billing](/help/plans-and-billing).

### What happens to my cards if I cancel Pro?

You keep everything — your library, review history, and sentences. Your account moves to the free plan when your paid period ends.

### Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Go to **Settings** › **Your data** and select **Export my data**. See [Managing your account and data](/help/account-and-data).

### Do you use my notes to train AI?

No. Your captures and study content are not used to train AI models. Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
