# Setting aside, fixing, or deleting a card

Sometimes a card isn't useful — maybe it's a name you'll never need, or the definition looks wrong. You can **set it aside** during a review to take it out of rotation. You can then bring it back, fix it, or delete it from your library.

Setting a card aside doesn't count as a grade. Your streak and accuracy aren't affected.

## Set a card aside during a review

1. While the card is on screen, select **Set aside**, or press **S**.
2. Choose a reason:
   - **I don't need this card** (or press **1**) — parks the card.
   - **The info looks wrong** (or press **2**) — parks the card, and for vocabulary, KanjiKatch also tries to fix the definition in the background.

The card leaves your review queue right away, including any remaining appearances in the current session.

Changed your mind? Select **Undo set aside** (or press **U**) right away to bring it back.

## Find your set-aside cards

1. Select **Library**.
2. Select the **Kanji** or **Vocabulary** tab.
3. Select **Set aside**. The number next to it shows how many cards are waiting.

> **Note:** The **In collection** / **Set aside** / **Deleted** switcher appears only after you've set aside at least one card on that tab.

Each set-aside card shows the reason you gave and when.

## Put a card back into your reviews

1. In the **Set aside** view, find the card.
2. Select **Put back**.

The card returns to your reviews and picks up its schedule where it left off.

## Fix a wrong definition

For a vocabulary card you set aside with **The info looks wrong**:

1. In the **Set aside** view, find the card.
2. Select **Fix definition**.
3. When the definition updates, check it. If it looks right, select **Put back**.

If a vocabulary card in your library says **Definition never loaded**, select **Fix now**.

## Delete a card

You can only delete a card after you've set it aside.

1. Set the card aside during a review (see above).
2. In **Library**, open the **Set aside** view.
3. Select **Delete** on the card.

A deleted card won't come back, even if you capture the same word again later.

## Restore a deleted card

1. In **Library**, select the **Kanji** or **Vocabulary** tab.
2. Select **Deleted**.
3. Select **Restore** on the card.
