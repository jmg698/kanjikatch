# Reading practice: See It In The Wild

Flashcards help you remember words. Reading them in real sentences helps you *understand* them. After each review session, KanjiKatch writes short, natural Japanese sentences using the words you just practiced.

## Read sentences after a review

1. Finish a review session (or select **Exit** to end it early).
2. On the summary screen, select **See It In The Wild**.
3. Read the first sentence in Japanese. Try to understand it before checking anything.
4. If you're stuck, select **Show Reading** to see the reading.
5. Select **Show Translation** to check your understanding.
6. Rate the sentence: **Too Easy**, **Just Right**, or **Too Hard**.
7. Move to the next sentence by swiping, using the arrows, or pressing **→** or **Space**.
8. On the last sentence, select **Finish Reading**.

> **Why rate sentences?** Your ratings teach KanjiKatch what "just right" feels like for you. After you've rated a few, new sentences are adjusted to match your level. Ratings don't affect your flashcard schedule.

On the free plan you get 2 sentences after each session. Pro gives you more. See *Free vs Pro: plans, limits, and billing*.

## Add a new word from a sentence

Words you've already studied are highlighted. Words you haven't studied yet can be tapped.

1. Tap a word in the sentence.
2. Check its reading and meaning in the popup.
3. Select **Add to My Vocabulary**.

The word is added to your library as a new card and will show up in your reviews. If the definition isn't ready yet, you'll see **Added! Definition will be filled in shortly.** — it'll be filled in automatically.

## Revisit your sentences

Every sentence KanjiKatch writes for you is saved.

1. Select **Read** in the navigation bar.
2. Browse your **Sentence Library**, or search by Japanese, English, or target word.
3. Use the sort menu to show **Recently added**, **Oldest first**, or **Alphabetical**.

## Practice with random sentences

1. Select **Read**.
2. Select **Review Sentences** (**Review** on phones).
3. Read through 10 random sentences from your library.
4. On the last one, select **More Sentences** for another batch, or **Done** to finish.

## Troubleshooting

**The See It In The Wild card doesn't appear**
Sentence generation sometimes fails when our AI is busy. Your review is still saved. Try again after your next session.

**"No sentences yet" on the Read page**
Sentences are created after review sessions. Select **Start a Review** and complete a session.

**"Daily generation limit reached"**
You've hit the daily limit on new sentences. Try again tomorrow.
