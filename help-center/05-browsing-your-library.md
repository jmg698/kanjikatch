# Browsing your library

Your library holds everything you've saved from your captures. Use it to look things up, check your progress, and manage cards you've set aside.

## Open your library

Select **Library** in the navigation bar. Your items are grouped into four tabs:

- **Kanji**
- **Vocabulary**
- **Grammar**
- **Sentences**

Each tab shows how many items it contains.

## What each item shows

- **Kanji:** the character, its meanings, on'yomi (**On**), kun'yomi (**Kun**), stroke count, JLPT level, and review stage. A **×N** badge means you've caught it in more than one capture.
- **Vocabulary:** the word, its reading, meanings, part of speech, JLPT level, and review stage.
- **Grammar:** the pattern, its structure, an explanation, and examples.
- **Sentences:** the Japanese, the English translation, and which capture it came from.

Items from the welcome-tour sample are marked **guided sample**.

## Search

1. Select a tab.
2. Type in the search box. You can search kanji and vocabulary by character, reading, or meaning; grammar by pattern or meaning; and sentences in Japanese or English.

To clear your search, select the **X** in the search box.

## Filter by JLPT level or review stage

1. Select the filter button (the sliders icon) next to the search box.
2. Choose one or more options:
   - **JLPT Level:** N5, N4, N3, N2, N1
   - **SRS Stage:** New, Learning, Reviewing, Known (kanji and vocabulary only)
3. To remove a filter, select the **X** on its chip. To remove all of them, select **Clear all**.

## Sort

Use the sort menu to order items by:

- **Recently seen** (or **Recently added** for sentences)
- **Oldest first**
- **Alphabetical**
- **Next review** (kanji and vocabulary only)
- **JLPT N5 → N1** or **JLPT N1 → N5**

The library shows 50 items at a time. Select **Load more** at the bottom to see more.

## Add new words to your library

There are two ways to add items:

- **Capture a page.** See *Capturing Japanese from photos and text*.
- **Tap a word in a practice sentence.** See *Reading practice: See It In The Wild*.
