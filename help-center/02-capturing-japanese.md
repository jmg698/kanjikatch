# Capturing Japanese from photos and text

Capturing is how you add material to KanjiKatch. You give it a photo or some text, and it pulls out the kanji, vocabulary, grammar patterns, and sentences for you to save.

## What you can capture

- Photos of handwritten notes or printed pages
- Screenshots (apps, websites, games, manga)
- Japanese text you paste or type

**Image requirements:** JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 4 MB, one image at a time.
**Text limit:** up to 20,000 characters per capture.

## Capture on your phone

1. Select **Capture** in the navigation bar.
2. Choose one of the three options:
   - **Take Photo** — opens your camera. Snap the page.
   - **Choose Photo** — pick an image from your photo library.
   - **Paste or Type** — paste text (or an image) into the box, or type directly.
3. Check the preview. To pick a different image, select **Change**.
4. Select **Extract Content**.

## Capture on a computer

1. Select **Capture** in the navigation bar.
2. Do one of the following:
   - Drag an image onto the box.
   - Paste an image or text into the box.
   - Select **browse files** to choose an image.
   - Type Japanese text directly.
3. Select **Extract Content**, or press **⌘ + Enter** (Mac) / **Ctrl + Enter** (Windows).

> **Note:** A capture is either an image *or* text, not both. Adding an image clears any text in the box, and typing clears an attached image.

## Check what KanjiKatch found

After a few seconds, the **Review what we found** screen shows everything KanjiKatch caught, grouped into **Kanji**, **Vocabulary**, **Grammar patterns**, and **Sentences**. Everything starts out selected.

1. **Uncheck** anything you don't want to study. Use **Select all** / **Deselect all** to change a whole section at once.
2. **Fix** anything that looks wrong:
   - Kanji: edit the meaning (**Mean**), on'yomi (**On**), and kun'yomi (**Kun**). Separate multiple entries with commas.
   - Vocabulary: edit the reading and meaning.
   - Grammar: edit the pattern, its **Form**, and what it **Means**.
   - Sentences: edit the Japanese or the English translation.
3. To drop an item completely, hover over it and select the trash icon.
4. Select **Save _N_ items to library**.

If a word is already in your library, KanjiKatch merges it with your existing card instead of creating a duplicate. Items saved with a blank meaning are skipped.

To start over without saving, select **Discard**.

## After you save

You'll see a summary of what was added, with how many items are new and how many you'd seen before. From here you can:

- **Create study guide** — turn this capture into a printable study guide.
- **Capture More** — add another page.
- **Review** — start reviewing your cards.
- **View Library** — browse what you've saved.

## Tips for better results

- Use good, even lighting and avoid shadows across the page.
- Crop close to the Japanese you care about.
- Hold the camera straight above the page, not at an angle.
- For long texts, capture one page or section at a time.

## Troubleshooting

**"No Japanese content found"**
KanjiKatch couldn't read any kanji, vocabulary, or sentences. Try a clearer photo, better lighting, or cropping closer to your notes, then select **Try Again**.

**"Something went wrong"**
Select **Try Again**. If it keeps happening, select **Report this issue**, add a short note about what you were capturing, and select **Send report**. Your report includes a reference code that helps us find the problem.

**"You're going a bit fast" or "temporarily unavailable"**
Wait a minute and try again.

**"You've used your 5 extractions for this month"**
You're out of free extractions for the month. See *Free vs Pro: plans, limits, and billing*.
