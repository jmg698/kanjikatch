# Managing your account and data

You're in control of your KanjiKatch account and everything in it. This article covers how to change your sign-in details, download your data, and delete your account.

## Change your email or password

1. Select your profile picture in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Manage account**.
3. Update your email address or password.

## Download your data

You can download a copy of your data at any time.

1. Select **Settings**.
2. Under **Your data**, select **Export my data**.

A JSON file named `kanjikatch-export-` followed by today's date downloads to your device. It includes your library (kanji, vocabulary, grammar, and sentences), your review history, your practice sentences, and your account profile.

## Remove the welcome-tour sample

If you borrowed the sample page during the welcome tour, you can remove it from your captures.

1. Select **Settings**.
2. Under **Onboarding**, find **Remove guided sample cards**, then select **Remove**.
3. Select **Remove** again to confirm.

Cards you saved from the sample stay in your library. To remove them, set each one aside during a review and delete it. See *Setting aside, fixing, or deleting a card*.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your library, sentences, review history, and captured images. **This can't be undone.**

> **Important:** Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription. If you have Pro, cancel it first so you aren't charged again. See *Free vs Pro: plans, limits, and billing*.

1. (Optional) Download your data first — see above.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Under **Danger zone**, select **Delete account**.
4. Type **DELETE** in the box.
5. Select **Delete my account permanently**.

You'll be signed out, and your data is deleted right away.

## How we handle your content

- The photos and text you capture are processed by AI to pull out the Japanese. The words you review are also used to write your practice sentences.
- Your captures and study content are **not** used to train AI models.
- We don't sell your data.

For full details, read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Report a problem or get help

- **If a capture fails:** on the **Something went wrong** screen, select **Report this issue**, describe what happened, and select **Send report**.
- **For anything else:** email **support@kanjikatch.com**.
