# Tracking your streak and progress

Your dashboard shows how your studying is going: what's due, how many days in a row you've studied, and which kanji need extra attention.

## Open your dashboard

Select **Home** in the navigation bar.

## What's on your dashboard

- **Ready for Review** — how many cards are due now, split into kanji and vocabulary. When nothing is due, you'll see **All Caught Up**.
- **Day streak** — how many days in a row you've reviewed. It appears once your streak reaches 2 days.
- **Kanji** and **Vocab** counts — how many items are in your library.
- **Worth Another Look** — up to six kanji you've been finding tricky, each with your accuracy. These are good candidates for extra attention.
- **Recently Added** — the last 20 kanji you've caught, each with a colored dot showing how well you know it. Select **View all →** to open your library.

## How your streak works

- Your streak goes up by one for each day you review at least one card.
- If you miss a day, your streak starts again at 1 the next time you review.
- Days are counted in **UTC** (Coordinated Universal Time), not your local time zone. Depending on where you live, the day may roll over in the afternoon or evening.
- Setting a card aside doesn't count for or against your streak.

> **Tip:** Even a 5-card session keeps your streak going. On the dashboard, select **Customize session**, then choose **5**.

Your streak also appears at the end of each review session.

## How to tell if you "know" a card

Each card moves through four stages as you review it: **New**, **Learning**, **Reviewing**, and **Known**. A card is **Known** once it's spaced more than three weeks apart and you get it right at least 85% of the time.

To see your cards by stage:

1. Select **Library**.
2. Select the **Kanji** or **Vocabulary** tab.
3. Select the filter button (the sliders icon).
4. Under **SRS Stage**, choose a stage.
