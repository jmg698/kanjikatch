# How reviews work

KanjiKatch uses **spaced repetition**: cards you know well come back less often, and cards you struggle with come back sooner. A few minutes of review each day is all it takes.

## Start a review session

**From the dashboard:**

1. Select **Home**.
2. In the **Ready for Review** panel, select **Start Review** to review everything that's due.

To review a smaller batch instead:

1. Select **Customize session**.
2. Choose **5**, **15**, **25**, **50**, or **All**.

**From anywhere:** select **Review** in the navigation bar. The badge next to it shows how many cards are due.

If nothing is due, you'll see **All Caught Up** — come back later.

## Review a card

1. Look at the kanji or word on the front of the card and try to recall the answer.
   - On vocabulary cards, you can select **Show reading** for a furigana hint.
2. Tap the card or press **Space** to reveal the answer.
3. Grade yourself honestly:

| Button | Key | Use it when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Again** | 1 | You forgot completely |
| **Hard** | 2 | You barely remembered |
| **Good** | 3 | You knew it |
| **Easy** | 4 | You knew it instantly |

The next card appears right away.

## Meaning and reading are practiced separately

Each kanji and word has two separate things to learn: its **meaning** and its **reading**. KanjiKatch schedules them independently, so you might be asked for a word's meaning today and its reading next week. After you reveal a card, a small label under the prompt tells you which one was being tested.

## When cards come back

- **New cards** come back after 1 day, then after 3 days. After that, the gap keeps growing — a week, a few weeks, then months.
- **Easy** stretches the gap further. **Hard** keeps it shorter.
- **Again** sends the card back to 1 day.

**Missed cards return in the same session.** If you choose **Again**, the card comes back a few cards later, and you need to get it right twice in a row to clear it. If you choose **Hard**, it comes back once. A **Quick recheck** banner tells you why you're seeing it. Only your first answer for each card affects its schedule.

## Card stages

As you review, each card moves through four stages:

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Not reviewed yet |
| **Learning** | You've started — it's still coming back often |
| **Reviewing** | You've recalled it correctly several times |
| **Known** | It's spaced more than three weeks apart and you get it right at least 85% of the time |

Missing a **Known** card moves it back to **Reviewing**. A card's overall stage is based on whichever of its two parts (meaning or reading) is weaker.

## Undo a grade

Tapped the wrong button? Select **Redo last card** or press **U** (or **←**) to undo your last answer and grade the card again. You can only undo your most recent answer.

## Reading breaks

In longer sessions, KanjiKatch pauses every 10 cards to show you a short sentence built from words you're reviewing. Read it, then select **Continue review**.

## End a session

When you finish, you'll see how many cards you got right, your accuracy, how long you spent, and your streak. From there you can:

- **See It In The Wild** — read sentences built from what you just reviewed.
- **Review Again** — start another session if more cards are due.
- **Back to Dashboard**

To stop early, select **Exit** at the top of the screen. Your progress so far is saved.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** during a review to see the shortcuts at any time.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Reveal the answer |
| 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 | Again / Hard / Good / Easy |
| S | Set the card aside |
| U or ← | Redo last card |
| ? | Show or hide shortcuts |
