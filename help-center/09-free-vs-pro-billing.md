# Free vs Pro: plans, limits, and billing

KanjiKatch is free for your daily review habit. Pro removes the limit on captures and gives you more reading practice.

## What's included

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviews | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Extractions (captures) | 10 welcome extractions, plus 5 per month | Unlimited (fair use) |
| Sentences after each review | 2 | Up to 5 |
| Study guides | ✓ | ✓ |
| Price | Free | $10/month or $100/year |

Pro comes with a **7-day free trial**.

## How free extractions work

An **extraction** is one capture — a photo or a block of text — that KanjiKatch reads for you.

- **Welcome bonus:** you get 10 extractions when you sign up. They never expire and are used first.
- **Monthly allowance:** you get 5 extractions each month. They refill on the 1st of each month (UTC) and don't roll over.
- Your first capture during the welcome tour is free.
- An extraction only counts if KanjiKatch successfully reads your capture. Failed captures don't count.

### Check how many extractions you have left

1. Select **Plan** in the navigation bar.
2. Look at the **Extractions** card. It shows your remaining **Monthly allowance**, when it refills, and your remaining **Welcome bonus**.

When you're running low, a banner also appears on the **Capture** page.

### When you run out

You'll see **You've used your free extractions for this month.** You can keep reviewing your cards as usual. To capture more, wait until your allowance refills on the 1st, or upgrade to Pro.

## Start a Pro free trial

1. Select **Plan**, then select **Upgrade to Pro**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**. Annual saves 17%.
3. Select **Start 7-day free trial**.
4. Enter your card details on the secure checkout page and confirm.

You'll return to KanjiKatch with Pro unlocked. A card is required to start the trial. If you cancel before the 7 days are up, you won't be charged.

## Update your payment method or view invoices

1. Select **Plan**.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. In the secure Stripe portal, update your card or download invoices.

## Cancel Pro

1. Select **Plan**.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. In the Stripe portal, select **Cancel subscription** and confirm.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for. Your **Plan** page shows the date your access ends. After that, your account moves to the free plan.

**You don't lose anything when you cancel.** Your library, cards, review history, and sentences all stay.

## Resume a canceled subscription

If you've canceled but your Pro access hasn't ended yet:

1. Select **Plan**.
2. Select **Resume subscription**.

## Fair use on Pro

"Unlimited" is meant for personal study, not automated use. If you hit the fair-use limit, you'll see a message asking you to get in touch — email **support@kanjikatch.com** and we'll raise it.
