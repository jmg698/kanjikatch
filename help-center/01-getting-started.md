# Getting started with KanjiKatch

KanjiKatch turns the Japanese you actually read — your class notes, a textbook page, a screenshot, a manga panel — into flashcards you review over time. This article walks you through your first few minutes.

## How KanjiKatch works

Everything in KanjiKatch follows one simple loop:

1. **Capture** a page of Japanese (a photo or pasted text).
2. **Check** what KanjiKatch found and save it to your library.
3. **Review** your new cards with spaced repetition.
4. **Read** short, natural sentences built from the words you just reviewed.

## Take the welcome tour

When you sign up, KanjiKatch starts a short welcome tour. It takes about three minutes.

1. On the **Catch your first.** screen, select **Show me**.
2. On the **Snap one page.** screen, choose where your first page comes from:
   - Select **Take a photo**, **Paste a screenshot**, or **Pick from library** to use your own material. This first capture is free — it doesn't count toward your monthly extractions.
   - Or, under **Or borrow one of ours.**, select the **Town News — the Nakano cat** sample, then select **Borrow this one**.
3. Wait a few seconds while KanjiKatch catches the kanji and vocabulary on the page.
4. On the **Review what we caught.** screen, uncheck anything you don't want and fix anything that looks wrong. Then select **Save _N_ items to library**.
5. Review your first five cards. For each card, tap it (or press **Space**) to reveal the answer, then choose how well you knew it.
6. Read a sentence or two that uses the words you just reviewed.
7. On the **That's the loop.** screen, choose **Go to dashboard**, **Capture more**, or **Keep reviewing**.

> **Tip:** If you've used KanjiKatch before, select **I've used KanjiKatch before — skip this.** on the first screen, or **Skip for now** on the second. If you leave the tour partway through, a **Resume tour →** link appears at the top of the app so you can pick up where you left off.

## Find your way around

The navigation bar at the top of every page (a second row on phones) has these tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your dashboard: cards due, streak, and recently added kanji |
| **Library** | Everything you've captured: kanji, vocabulary, grammar, and sentences |
| **Guides** | Printable study guides built from your captures |
| **Read** | Your collection of practice sentences |
| **Plan** | Your subscription and remaining extractions |
| **Settings** | Your account, data export, and account deletion |
| **Capture** | Add a new page of Japanese |
| **Review** | Start reviewing. The badge shows how many cards are due. |

## Replay the tour later

1. Select **Settings**.
2. Under **Onboarding**, find **Replay onboarding tour**, then select **Replay**.

Replaying the tour doesn't change your existing library.
